Lab 1 · Part D · Pseudocode
Write an algorithm for ONE of these, as numbered steps, the way we wrote the phone-book algorithm in class:
  (a) find the largest number in a stack of exam papers, one paper at a time;
  (b) make a cup of tea for a robot that knows nothing;
  (c) guess a number between 1 and 100 that a friend is thinking of, using yes/no questions.
Rules: at least 6 numbered lines; use at least one "if" and one loop ("go back to line N", "repeat" or "while").
Replace the example below with your own.

1  Pick up the first paper
2  Remember its number as "largest so far"
3  If there are no papers left, go to line 7
4  Pick up the next paper
5  If its number is bigger than "largest so far", remember it as "largest so far"
6  Go back to line 3
7  Announce "largest so far"


The answer for option B
1-Go to the kitchen.
2-Look for the jar named "Tea"
3-If you can not find it , announce: THE MATERILAS FOR THE TEA COULDNT BEEN FOUND.TRY AGAIN LATER" and stop there.
4-Take it and put it on the table.
5-Go near the drawer and open it.
6-Take a spoon and put it near the jar.
7-Go and find the kettle.
8-Get the kettle and go near to the water tap.
9-Turn the water tap and fill the kettle with water. 
10-When it reaches 500mL , turn the tap off and take the kettle with you. 
11-Put it on the stove.
12-Light up the stove.
13-Wait until the water boils.
14-When the water boils , go near to the jar and spoon.
15-Take them with you and put it somewhere near the stove.
16-Open the jar and take 2 full spoons of tea.
17-Open the lid of the kettle and pour the tea.
18-Close the kettles lid and turn off the stove.
19-Close the tea jar and set a timer for 8 minutes.
20-Loop the music 'Shape Of My Heart' until the timer ends.
21-When the timer runs out stop playing music.
22-Announce "The tea is ready.Bon appetit."
